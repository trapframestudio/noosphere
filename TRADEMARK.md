## Why this exists

Noosphere's **code is open source** under [MIT](LICENSE-MIT) / [Apache 2.0](LICENSE-APACHE), and you are
welcome — encouraged — to build new games on the engine and systems. The
**Noosphere name and brand are not open.** This policy exists so that the
open-source code can be freely reused while players can still trust that
anything called "Noosphere" is the official game.

In short: **use the code, build your own thing, but call it your own thing.**

## Marks covered

- The name **"Noosphere"** as it refers to this game/project.
- Noosphere **logos** and wordmarks (see `godot/assets/logos/`).
- The names of the in-world factions, places, and organizations that form the
  Noosphere identity (e.g. *Pacific Workers' Alliance*, *Revere's Guard*,
  *Gulf Compact*, *Federal*, *The Merged*, *Project Citizen*), to the extent
  they function as brand identifiers of the game.

## What you may do

- Use the name "Noosphere" **nominatively** to refer truthfully to this project
  — e.g. "a mod for Noosphere", "built on the Noosphere engine", "contributes
  to Noosphere".
- State accurately that your separate project **uses code from the Noosphere
  open-source repository**, provided you do not imply endorsement or affiliation.
- Use the marks in unmodified copies of the official game and its
  documentation.

## What you may not do (without written permission)

- Name a **fork, derived game, or other product** "Noosphere" or anything
  confusingly similar.
- Use the Noosphere name, logos, or faction/world names as the **brand or
  identity** of another game or product.
- Imply that your project is **official, endorsed by, or affiliated with**
  Noosphere or `[ENTITY NAME]` when it is not.
- Use the marks in a way that is misleading, disparaging, or that dilutes the
  brand.

## Forks and derived games

If you build a new game on the Noosphere open-source code (which [MIT](LICENSE-MIT) /
[Apache 2.0](LICENSE-APACHE) permit), you must:

1. **Rename and rebrand.** Choose your own name, logo, and identity.
2. **Bring your own creative content.** See the
   [Noosphere Content License](LICENSE-ASSETS.md) — the art, audio, world, and
   story are not yours to ship.
3. **Not imply affiliation** with the official Noosphere project.
