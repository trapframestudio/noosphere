# AI Usage

We use AI tools. This isn't a secret, and we're not going to pretend otherwise or write a laundry list of exactly where it's allowed. The landscape moves too fast for that to hold up anyway.

What we will do is be straight about the principle behind it, because this project deserves that, and so does the community building it with us.

---

## What we're actually building

Noosphere is its own game. It's an original co-op survival experience set in an affected stretch of the Columbia River Gorge. The horror is American, the failure is American, and the tone is closer to Strugatsky than to slasher. We love S.T.A.L.K.E.R.: Anomaly and the GAMMA modpack, and they shaped how we think about systems-driven survival sims, but Noosphere is not a clone, port, or recreation of any of that. The world, factions, story, and art are original, and we are building it for co-op from the ground up.

That means we're making our own creative decisions. Some systems will feel familiar. Some will be reimagined entirely. The atmosphere we are chasing is quiet, patient, indifferent - the world does not care that you are here, and a second player does not soften it.

---

## The principle

> AI is a tool, and we'll use it where it helps us ship something worth playing. The one thing we won't do is use it in ways that compromise the feel of what we're building. The atmosphere of a survival sim is not a variable.

In practice that means AI has a real role in the engineering side of things: making sense of large codebases, generating boilerplate, prototyping systems, writing tests. The kind of work where it makes us faster without making creative calls on our behalf.

Where it doesn't have a role is in defining what the game feels like. That's a human call, made in conversation with the community. If something AI-generated would shift the atmosphere, the tone, or the soul of the experience in the wrong direction, it doesn't make it in. Full stop.

---

## Our commitments

- The feel we are chasing is sacred to us. No AI output ships if it compromises that, period.
- Anything AI-assisted gets reviewed and understood by a human before it hits the project. We don't merge what we can't explain.
- We'll be upfront when AI played a meaningful part in something.
- If you've got concerns about how AI was used somewhere specific, raise it. We'll take it seriously. This policy can change.
- These rules apply to stewards and maintainers the same as everyone else.

---

The survival sims that inspired this project have a specific kind of weight to them. That didn't happen by accident, it came from real creative decisions made by real people. We're here to build something new in that lineage, and we're not going to let a shortcut be the thing that hollows it out.

Open to amendment via community discussion.
