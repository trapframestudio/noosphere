## Why Noosphere needs a CLA

Most open-source projects don't require a CLA. Noosphere does, for two specific
reasons tied to how it's funded:

1. **The official game is sold commercially** (on Steam) and combines open code
   with proprietary content. To ship your contribution inside that commercial
   product, the project needs an explicit, clear grant of rights from you.
2. **Contributors can share in revenue and join the paid team.** To pay you, and
   to keep the project's ownership of its own work unambiguous, the project
   needs a clean rights chain. The CLA is what makes the
   [contributor revenue model](book/src/project/contributor-revenue.md) possible.

The CLA is a **license**, not an assignment: **you keep the copyright to your
contribution.** You grant the project broad rights to use it.

## Agreement (draft terms)

By contributing to Noosphere, You agree to the following with respect to all
past and future Contributions You submit to the project:

### 1. Definitions

- **"You"** means the individual who submits a Contribution.
- **"Project"** means the Noosphere project and `[ENTITY NAME]`, its maintainer.
- **"Contribution"** means any original work of authorship — code, documentation,
  designs, or other material — that You intentionally submit to the Project
  (e.g. via pull request, patch, or issue attachment).

### 2. Copyright license

You retain ownership of the copyright in Your Contribution. You grant the
Project and recipients of software distributed by the Project a **perpetual,
worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable** copyright license to
reproduce, prepare derivative works of, publicly display, publicly perform,
**sublicense, and distribute** Your Contribution and such derivative works,
**including as part of a commercial product** and under **license terms of the
Project's choosing** (including [MIT](../LICENSE-MIT) and [Apache 2.0](../LICENSE-APACHE)
and, where applicable, the Project's commercial or proprietary terms).

### 3. Patent license

You grant the Project and recipients a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive,
royalty-free, irrevocable (except as stated below) patent license to make, use,
sell, offer to sell, import, and otherwise transfer Your Contribution, where the
license applies to patent claims You can license that are necessarily infringed
by Your Contribution alone or by its combination with the Project. If any entity
institutes patent litigation alleging that the Project or a Contribution
constitutes patent infringement, the patent licenses granted under this CLA for
that party terminate.

### 4. You have the right to grant this

You represent that:

- Each Contribution is **Your original creation**, or You have the rights to
  submit it under these terms.
- If Your employer has rights to work You create, You have permission to make
  the Contribution on Your own behalf, or Your employer has waived such rights.
- Your Contribution **does not include any third-party game assets or other
  material You do not have the right to submit** (consistent with the project's
  long-standing "no third-party game assets, ever" rule).

### 5. No obligation; revenue share is separate

This CLA does **not** entitle You to compensation by itself, and does not
obligate the Project to use Your Contribution. Any revenue share or compensation
is **governed by a separate, signed Contributor Agreement** and is described in
principle in the [contributor revenue model](book/src/project/contributor-revenue.md).
Until that separate agreement is signed, any payment is discretionary.

### 6. No warranty

Unless required by law, You provide Your Contribution "AS IS" without warranties
of any kind.

## How to sign (intended process)

- For most contributors, the CLA bot will prompt You to accept on Your first
  pull request. Acceptance is recorded against Your GitHub identity.
- Corporate contributors (contributing on behalf of an employer) will need a
  separate Corporate CLA — `[to be drafted with counsel]`.

If you have questions about the CLA before it is finalized, open a discussion or
contact the project lead.
